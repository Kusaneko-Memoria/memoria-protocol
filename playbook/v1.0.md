# Memoria v1.0 — Playbook (Complete, Code-Accurate)

## 0) What this demo is
Memoria v1.0 is a single-player toy that lets you **feel** the difference between:

- **Non-returning loops** (spending that rarely comes back), and  
- **Returning loops** (actions that can trigger future resonance and opportunities),

without requiring you to “believe” any theory.

This build is **not**:
- a blockchain  
- a currency  
- a consensus protocol  

It **is**:
- an append-only event log  
- a post-hoc meaning toy  
- a minimal “resonance → return” simulator  

---

## 1) Core rules (as implemented)

### A) Append-only
All events go into `S.txs` and are never deleted.

### B) No Free Resonance (as text + behavior)
There is **no manual** “Attach Resonance” button.  
Resonance is only allowed to appear **after actions exist**, and it appears **probabilistically** on `Next Turn`.

### C) Hash-chained log (important detail)
Each transaction includes:
- `prev_tx_id`
- `tx_id = SHA256(JSON(tx) + "|" + prev_tx_id)`

So the log is a **hash chain**.

Not consensus, but you’ve embedded:
> “History should be tamper-evident.”

---

## 2) UI: What each number means

### Balance
Your current spendable resource (`S.balance`), starting at 100.

### Commit Mass (CM)
`S.cm` increments by +1 each time you do an action (even Help).  
Interpretation: **CM is “I acted.”**  
It does **not** mean the action mattered.

### Resonance Mass (RM)
`S.rm` increments only on turns where resonance appears.  
Interpretation: **RM is “the future referenced something you did.”**

### Soul Mass (SM)
Displayed as `CM + RM`.  
Interpretation: **Soul Mass is “I acted” + “the world echoed back.”**

### Turn
Discrete time steps.  
In v1.0, Turn advances **only** when you press `Next Turn ▶`.

---

## 3) The 3 action buttons (exact behavior)

### A) Subscription Spend (Non-returning)
- Cost: **8**
- Commits an `ACTION` and increases `CM`
- Not favored for resonance (only “any action” has a small chance later)

Meaning:
> Comfort is stable, but does not naturally accumulate resonance.

### B) Nice Roll Spend (Returning)
- Cost: **6**
- Marks action as `"NiceRoll"`
- Tracked in `lastRollActionIds`
- `Next Turn` has a higher chance (**45%**) to attach resonance to recent rolls

Meaning:
> You spend without expecting return → the system is biased to let resonance attach later.

### C) Help by Action (No Money)
- Cost: **0**
- Still creates `ACTION` and increases `CM`

Meaning:
> Meaning can attach even without money.

---

## 4) What happens on “Next Turn ▶” (the real engine)

When you click `Next Turn`:

1) **Turn +1**

2) **Resonance may appear**
- If you have recent `NiceRoll` actions → **45% chance** to add `RM += 1`
- Else (if any recent actions exist) → **10% chance** to add `RM += 1`

3) **If RM > 0, there’s a chance of returning opportunity**
- Chance grows with RM: `min(0.05 + 0.02*RM, 0.4)`
- If triggered: Balance gains **+2 to +5**

Core demonstration:
- Resonance is not created by “claiming meaning.”
- Resonance appears later, and can unlock returns.

---

## 5) Fast play recipes (what to do)

### Recipe A — Feel “non-returning loop”
- Spam `Subscription Spend` 6–8 times
- Click `Next Turn` repeatedly

Observe:
- balance drops steadily
- resonance appears rarely
- opportunities return infrequently

### Recipe B — Feel “returning loop”
- Mix `Nice Roll Spend` multiple times
- Click `Next Turn` several times

Observe:
- resonance attaches more often
- returns appear more often once `RM > 0`

### Recipe C — “meaning without money”
- Use `Help by Action` multiple times
- Click `Next Turn` repeatedly

Observe:
- resonance can still appear
- “meaning” does not require payment

## 6) One-sentence README explanation
v1.0 shows that meaning can be modeled as a delayed, probabilistic echo of actions — not something assigned at the moment of action.
