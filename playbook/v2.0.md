# Memoria v2.0 — Playbook (Complete, Code-Accurate)

## 0) What this demo is
v2.0 is the minimal “Actor vs Witness” separation:

- Actor commits an `ACTION`
- Witness later references an `ACTION`
- The ledger is append-only
- No scoring, no finalization

It is **pure post-hoc reference**.

---

## 1) Core rule: No Free Resonance (strict, enforced)
In `witness()`:

- First checks if any `ACTION` exists  
  - If none → “Nothing to witness.” and returns
- Otherwise creates a `RESONANCE` with:
  - `ref: target.id`

So resonance cannot exist without a prior action.

---

## 2) The hidden “turn” behavior (important!)
Your code increments `turn++` inside `render()`, and `render()` is called after every TX.

That means:
- Turn advances automatically after every append
- There is no `Next Turn` button by design
- Time is **event-based** rather than **tick-based**

---

## 3) Buttons (exact behavior)

### Commit Action
Creates:

- `type: "ACTION"`
- `id: crypto.randomUUID()`
- `turn`
- `note`

Then appends to ledger and renders.

### Witness Random Action
- Picks a random `ACTION` from ledger
- Creates:

  - `type: "RESONANCE"`
  - `ref: target.id`
  - `turn`
  - `note`

Then appends and renders.

---

## 4) What to observe
Resonance is nothing but a reference (`ref`) to an earlier action.

The ledger becomes an explicit graph:

- actions are nodes
- resonances are directed edges

This is the clean “protocol skeleton” view.

---

## 5) Fast play recipes

### Recipe A — Basic post-hoc meaning
- Click `Commit Action` 3 times
- Click `Witness Random Action` 5 times
- Notice the `ref:xxxxxx` in ledger lines

### Recipe B — “meaning is not intention”
- Create many actions
- Witness several times
- Some actions get referenced more often by chance

That alone creates “selection pressure,” even before scoring exists.
